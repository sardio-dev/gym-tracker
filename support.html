Support

Something not working, or an idea for the app? Write to hello@sardio.dev. A person reads it.

Keep a backup

Your data lives on your device only, so a backup is a file you keep. More › Backup & data › Export / backup makes one file with everything, and the share sheet saves it to Files, mail or Notes. The iPhone app also writes a backup every Sunday into its folder in the Files app and keeps the newest eight.

Move to a new phone

Export on the old phone and send the file over (AirDrop, mail, a shared folder). On the new phone, choose I have a backup on the first screen and open the file: workouts, weigh-ins, nutrition and settings are back before anything is asked.

Reminders do not show up

Allow notifications for Gym Tracker in the iPhone's Settings, then set the training days again in More › Reminders. A Focus mode can hold them back; the end of a rest is marked time sensitive so it can come through.

I deleted the app

Deleting the app deletes its data, and there is no copy anywhere else. If you exported a backup before, install the app again and restore it with I have a backup.
