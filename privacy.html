Privacy

Gym Tracker has no account, no server and no analytics. What you log stays on your device.

What the app stores

Your workouts, weights, body measurements, nutrition and sleep entries, notes and settings are stored on your device only: in the app's storage on the iPhone, or in your browser's storage when you use the web app. Nothing of it is sent to me or to anyone else. I cannot see it, and I cannot restore it for you.

  • Backups are files on your device. The automatic weekly backup is written to the app's folder in Files, and an export goes wherever you send it from the share sheet.
  • Reminders and the rest timer are local notifications, scheduled on the device. The app uses no push service.
  • There are no ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics and no sign-in.

The only connections the app makes

  • Update check (iPhone app). When it starts, the app asks Expo's update service (Expo, 650 Industries Inc., USA) whether a newer version of the app's own code is available, and downloads it if so. The request carries technical details only: the platform, the app's version, and a random installation ID that is created on the device and is not derived from your hardware, your Apple Account or anything you enter. As with any internet request, Expo's servers see your IP address. None of your entries are part of it. The purpose is to deliver fixes (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Without a connection the app simply starts as it is.
  • Web app. gym.sardio.dev is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA), which processes the IP addresses of visitors to deliver the site and keep it secure. After the first visit the app's files are kept in your browser and it works without a connection.

Your control

Everything is in your hands inside the app: More › Backup & data exports all of it as one file, and Delete all data in the same place erases it. Deleting the app, or the website's data in your browser, removes it as well. Because I hold no data about you, there is nothing I could hand over, correct or delete on my side; if you write to me, I only have your message.

Health information

The numbers the app suggests – weights, calories, protein, body fat estimates – are general training guidance worked out on your device from what you entered. They are not medical advice.
