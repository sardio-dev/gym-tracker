Terms

Overload is free to use. Pro is an optional unlock. These are the terms, in plain words.

The app

Overload is a training log with a fixed plan and advice on when to add weight. It has no account and no server: what you log stays on your device (see Privacy). The free version is complete – the plan, the timed session, History, Nutrition, backups, export and import, reminders, Apple Health and every language are free and stay free.

The numbers the app suggests – weights, calories, protein, body fat estimates – are general training guidance worked out from what you entered. They are not medical advice. Train within your ability and ask a doctor if you are unsure.

Pro

Pro adds the extras: no ads, ever; cloud file sync; and custom routines as they ship, plus every Pro feature that follows. Two ways to get it, both bought inside the app:

  • Lifetime: one payment. Pro stays unlocked for as long as the app exists, on every device signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account.
  • Yearly: a subscription that renews every year at the price shown in the app at the time you buy, until you cancel it. Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings; Pro then ends at the close of the paid year. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Prices are set in the stores and shown in your currency in the app before you buy. Payment, invoices and refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their terms; I do not see your payment details. To get Pro back on a new device, tap Restore purchases in More › Pro – a backup file does not carry it, the store account does.

Changes

The app and this page change as the app grows. A change to what Pro includes never takes away what you paid for: a lifetime purchase stays a lifetime purchase. If the app is ever withdrawn from the stores, it keeps working on your device with what it has.
